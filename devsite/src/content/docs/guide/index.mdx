Ephyra plays the movies and series of your own [Jellyfin](https://jellyfin.org/) server on
Android phones, tablets and Android TV. It is a fork of
[Findroid](https://github.com/jarnedemeulemeester/findroid): everything Findroid does, with
downloads worth using, Dolby Vision, tablet layouts and a TV app built for the remote on top.

It is free, licensed under the GPLv3, and collects nothing
([privacy policy](/ephyra/privacy.html)). It needs a Jellyfin server
(12.0 or newer), and Android 10 or newer on phones and tablets or Android 9 or newer on TV.
