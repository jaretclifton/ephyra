## Styling

Colour, outline, font and size are set in the player settings and apply on ExoPlayer and on
mpv. Styled tracks (SSA/ASS) keep their own look unless you switch on the override. On Android
TV the same choices are a list in Settings.

## Sync

When subtitles run early or late, move them from the subtitle menu in the player, in small
steps. On the phone and tablet you can also line a subtitle up with what is said.

## Remembered per series

Choose Japanese audio with English subtitles for one series, and the next episode starts that
way: on a stream, on a download and on both players. The choice is kept per series and per
user, so it does not change what someone else on the same device hears.
