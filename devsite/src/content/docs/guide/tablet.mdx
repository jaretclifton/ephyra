The tablet runs the phone app, laid out for the window rather than the device. A tablet, an
unfolded foldable, a split-screen half and a desktop window each get what fits, and everything
the phone app does is there.

## Foldables

<Shots
  shots={[
    { file: 'fold-tabletop.webp', alt: 'The player on a half-folded foldable: the video on the upper half, the transport controls and seek bar on the lower half', caption: 'Tabletop: video above the fold, controls below' },

Folded, a foldable is a phone. Unfolded it gets the tablet layouts, and half folded on a table
the player splits at the hinge.

For the design and the sizes it is tested at, see
[docs/tablet](https://github.com/midasvo/ephyra/tree/main/docs/tablet).
