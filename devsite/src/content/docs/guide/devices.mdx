Ephyra is two apps. The **phone app** also runs on tablets, foldables and desktop windows,
with [layouts for the width](/ephyra/guide/tablet/). The **TV app** is a separate one,
[built for the remote](/ephyra/guide/tv/). They share the player, the library code and the
login to your server.

## Per area

<Radar />

The TV app has no downloads and no Cast. The phone app has none of the remote-only features,
and the tablet layouts show from 600 dp of width.
