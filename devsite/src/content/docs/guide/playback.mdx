## Dolby Vision and Atmos

Dolby Vision plays directly where the device supports it. Where it doesn't, the server
converts it, so you get a picture instead of wrong colours. Dolby Atmos goes through the
device's own decoder; when a decoder fails, playback continues with ffmpeg instead of stopping.

Movie and episode pages show HDR10+ and Dolby Atmos badges, so you know what a version is
before it plays. About → Export device profile writes down what your device says it can play;
attach it to a [bug report](https://github.com/midasvo/ephyra/issues) about playback.
