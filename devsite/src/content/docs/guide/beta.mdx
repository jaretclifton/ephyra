Every change that lands on `main` publishes a prerelease, `v<next version>-beta.<n>`. It
installs as a separate app, **Ephyra Beta**, with its own login, settings and downloads, so it
does not touch the stable app.

<Aside type="caution">A beta is untested. Expect bugs, and keep the stable app for the evening's film.</Aside>

## With Obtainium

Add the repository again, as a second entry:

- Source URL `https://github.com/midasvo/ephyra`
- **Include prereleases** on
- **Filter release titles by regular expression**: `^Beta`. Without it the entry switches to
  the stable release right after one comes out.
- **Filter APKs by regular expression**: the line for your device.

## By hand

The prereleases are on the [releases page](https://github.com/midasvo/ephyra/releases), marked
**Pre-release**. The file names follow the [stable ones](/ephyra/guide/install/#which-file).

Found a bug in a beta? [Open an issue](https://github.com/midasvo/ephyra/issues) and say
which beta you run.
