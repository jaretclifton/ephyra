## External ratings

Movie and show pages can show the scores of IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, Metacritic and Letterboxd.
They come from [MDBList](https://mdblist.com/), not from your server, so this is off until you
switch it on in the settings and enter a free MDBList key of your own.

While it is on, opening a movie or show page sends that item's IMDb, TMDB or TVDB ID and your
key to mdblist.com. Nothing is sent while it is off, and the key stays on the device: it is left
out of backups and device transfers.

## When the server doesn't answer

Ephyra notices when your server stops answering: on a network change, when you return to the
app and after a failed request. On the phone and tablet a banner then says so, with a way to
your [downloads](/ephyra/guide/downloads/#without-the-server), and it goes by itself when the
server is back. Screens that fail to load say so and offer Retry.
