## Two colours with a meaning

- **Cyan means alive**: what is playing, downloading or in focus.
- **Violet means yours**: favorites, what you have watched, your ratings.

Everything else stays navy and quiet, so the artwork carries the colour. The design is written
down in the [identity document](/ephyra/dev/working/identity/).
