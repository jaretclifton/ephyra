A separate app in every release (the `-tv-` files, see [Install](/ephyra/guide/install/)), for
64-bit devices and for 32-bit boxes such as the Xiaomi Mi Box. It uses the same server and
login as the phone app, and it is built for the remote: everything is reachable with the D-pad,
and settings that only make sense on a phone are hidden.

## Shared with the phone app

Most of what the phone app adds is on TV too: Dolby Vision, the version picker, media
segments, the "Up next" card, the sleep timer, subtitle styling and sync, Home rows you choose,
hide watched, hide spoilers, fuller item pages and profiles with a PIN.

Not on TV: downloads, changing the quality while playing, Cast, background audio and Material
You colours. [What runs where](/ephyra/guide/devices/) has the whole list.

The decisions behind the TV app are in the
[TV decision records](/ephyra/dev/decisions/#tv).
